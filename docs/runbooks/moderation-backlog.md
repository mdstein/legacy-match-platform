# Moderation backlog

1. Triage reports with safety threats and platform abuse first, then cheating/smurfing evidence, griefing, and toxicity.
2. Claim a report before reviewing it. Use the match roster, signed events, canonical GOTV checksum, analyzer warnings, and surrounding rounds.
3. Record a concrete finding before dismissal or sanction. Never sanction another moderator/admin unless an administrator handles the case.
4. Review appeals independently where staffing permits. An overturned appeal deactivates the sanction and immediately refreshes queue eligibility.
5. Track oldest pending age and reviewer throughput. Add trained moderators before relaxing evidence standards.
